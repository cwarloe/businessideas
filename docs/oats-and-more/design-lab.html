What each direction tests

1. Oats, Etc.

Question: Does a warm, neighborhood-friendly brand make unfamiliar savory oat dishes feel approachable?
Tradeoff: Less immediate “build-a-bowl” energy.

2. Oatmeal Station

Question: Do clear ordering steps and the word “oatmeal” improve immediate comprehension?
Tradeoff: The name may sound breakfast-only.

3. Oats All Ways

Question: Does vivid, playful branding encourage people to try unexpected savory bowls?
Tradeoff: Less restrained and potentially less established-feeling.

All three names are exploration candidates, not trademark-cleared marks. The current live poll remains on the existing Oats, Etc. website; this design lab does not collect votes or alter the Cloudflare database.
