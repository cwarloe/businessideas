# Oats, Etc. — experiments, decision gates and open items

**Status: not yet executed as fieldwork.** This is the current action backlog and evidence log as of November 11, 2026.

## Working hypothesis

> An office complex with a supportive building manager and legal electricity access can regularly generate **30–60 paid oatmeal breakfasts** in a **three-hour morning window** from a single, simply outfitted station operated by one person.

To test this, maximize **verified demand per setup hour**, not the attractiveness of the vehicle. Initial stretch target: 30 bowls sold **before 9 a.m.**; possible 40–60 bowls over 6:30–9:30 a.m.

## Experiment sequence

| Priority | Experiment or research action | Evidence that counts | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Identify 3–5 Boise-area office/business campuses with high morning arrival count | Names/addresses, approximate staff/visitors, host contacts, entry traffic observations, permissions | Where to pilot |
| **P0** | Interview host/site managers about hosting a recurring oatmeal pop-up | Written willingness, location and hours, accessible power supply, expected fees, promotion to occupants | Does a suitable private site exist? |
| **P0** | Map legal land boundary and zoning for top site | Parcel map, private/ROW boundary, City/County classification | Is recurring vending lawful there? |
| **P0** | Ask CDH to classify a hot-oatmeal-only counter / cart / ultralight towable setup | CDH response, equipment/handwash/waste/commissary list, feasible permit path | What is the minimum legal unit? |
| **P1** | Offer employees a short survey and/or **actual refundable preorder invitation** (only if lawful and feasible) | Number of real people who opt in, likely frequency and price acceptance | Does demand reach 30+ bowls? |
| **P1** | Run permitted morning service test or supervised sample where appropriate | Actual sold count, timeline, payment success, delays, repeats | Test one-operator throughput |
| **P1** | Collect customer preference feedback from paying customers | Classic vs maple/nuts vs apple preferences, portion complaints, price reactions | Finalize 3-bowl starter menu |
| **P1** | Price and weigh every approved equipment component (including water) | Vendor quotations, scale weights, electrical specification, safety rating | Portable SUV station vs trailer |
| **P2** | Trial 10-bowl office preorder bundle and a premium crunch topping | Actual take rate and increased ticket, packaging/assembly time | Low-friction average-ticket growth |
| **P2** | Test breakfast frequency (3/4/5 days) | Repeat purchases per day, host demand and waste | Work schedule |
| **P3** | Lunch-only presale test with 2 savory menu concepts | 15–25 **genuine** lunch preorders on trial days and approved food handling | Whether to add lunch |
| **P3** | Evaluate second location/second stand | Stable sales, repeatable processes, supervision needs, logistics | Scaling rather than just working longer |

*Targets are working thresholds for evidence collection, not promises or automatic permit/business approvals.*

## Measurements to capture from day one

- Date, start/end time, host/location, weather, electrical arrangement.
- Starting cooked-oatmeal cups, serving sizes and topping stock; leftover/discarded cups.
- Bowls sold by SKU/size, addons, gross sales, paid order counts, refund count, card/cash totals.
- Orders arriving by 15-minute interval; max queue / longest service time.
- Customer type (walk-up/preorder/group), repeat customers (voluntary/anonymous), qualitative feedback.
- Setup, service and teardown minutes; travel distance; any safety issues.
- Ingredient, packaging and other variable expense records (even though initial forecast excludes labor).

## Decision gates

### 1 → 2: Invest beyond the simplest pop-up

**Candidate trigger:** ~30+ real paid bowls during a morning **on multiple weeks**, clear host support, meaningful repeat demand, functioning safe food process, and evidence the portable setup is the constraint. Test whether a trailer reduces setup labor enough to justify its costs and towing risk.

### 2 → 3: Add lunch

**Candidate trigger:** sustained 40–60 morning bowls without operational bottlenecks, and a credible test of **15–25 preordered lunch bowls** on selected days. Validate lunch-specific menu, safe holding, and whether the added daypart increases total sales more than it increases complexity.

### 3 → replication

**Candidate trigger:** stable demand at one site, documented procedures for ingredients, hot-holding and serving, permits and food safety well understood, and clear per-location sales/variable costs. A second site should be a repeatable operation, not simply a longer solo workday.

## Explicit unresolved questions

### Brand
- Have we completed federal/state/common-law clearance for **Oats, Etc.** and competing name variants?
- Which primary tagline will be legally cleared and actually preferred by consumers?
- Is `oatsetc.com` registered and in the right Cloudflare account, and does it point to the correct website?
- Is an OATS & MORE IP deal worth discussing, given a different working brand?
- Should the design lab be used for a structured poll (separate question) rather than just informal visual feedback?

### Food/menu
- Actual recipe, oatmeal type, texture stability and safety after 2–3 hours in hot holding?
- Serving size: **8, 10 or 12 fl oz of cooked oatmeal** plus toppings?
- Is fruit topping handled fresh, cooked, or prepacked? How do we manage refrigeration if required?
- Does the menu require nut-free preparation tools or dedicated packaging and allergen signage?
- Real ingredient and package costs per bowl and waste; is $5.95–$7.95 pricing viable?
- Is a packaged beverage worth the storage, or simply focus on premium oat toppings?

### Site and permits
- Which **specific private commercial parcel** has 30–60 likely morning buyers?
- Written property owner permission, parking/ADA clearance, private vs ROW line?
- City vendor and zoning treatment for recurring 6:30 a.m. station?
- CDH approval details: kitchen/commissary, preparation/reheating, water/handwash, wastewater and hot holding?
- Host electrical load capacity, safe cable route, use fee, insurance requirements?

### Hardware and vehicle
- Does the **2017 Nissan Rogue** have a receiver, wiring and adequate tow configuration? Exact trim/FWD/AWD?
- Does the entire station fit **inside** the Rogue without compromise to transport safety?
- For an ultralight trailer, what do an actual vendor's complete loaded weight, tongue load, brakes and registration show?
- What number/size of **warmers** will serve projected bowls without line delays or food loss?
- How much setup time does a bike/trike save relative to a portable kiosk? Or is it purely a marketing expense?
- When might a cottage-style food trailer justify changing tow vehicles or arranging separate delivery?

### Demand and economics
- Is the target achievable across multiple mornings, not just a novelty event?
- How concentrated is arrival traffic between 6:30 and 9:30 a.m.? Can one person meet bursts?
- Does the host promote the stand internally, and are repeat orders measurable?
- What premium topping bundles increase the average ticket without reducing throughput?
- What operating model allows expansion while keeping food handling and service simple?

## Preservation rule

Do **not** delete alternatives when one direction becomes favored: mark them *deferred*, *rejected* or *replaced* and include a brief reason. The point of these records is to save the assumptions and learnings that led to each choice. Update the **decision log** when a new fact changes the plan, and add dated results here when a field experiment or authority conversation actually occurs.
