**Last consolidated:** November 11, 2026.  
**Scope:** All the naming, concept-design, market, food-cart, trailer, food-service, and sales-model brainstorming in the current development conversation.  
**Status:** Working hypotheses, **not** an operating plan cleared by a trademark attorney, City of Boise, Central District Health, property owner, or trailer manufacturer.

## Read this first

Oats, Etc. is a proposed **warm, familiar oatmeal breakfast service** (sweet bowls first, rather than eggs/griddle cooking) targeting **a recurring morning location with concentrated workers**, rather than a food truck that roams in search of traffic. A host willing to welcome the business and provide **lawful power on private commercial property** is preferable. The **2017 Nissan Rogue's modest towing limit** now makes a lightweight transportable station more attractive than an enclosed concession trailer, at least for proof of concept.

The next experiment is to validate an office-complex breakfast location and actual demand before purchasing a bicycle, trailer, or specialized kiosk.

## Operating principles

- **No premature equipment purchase.** Find demand and a compliant host/location first.
- **Food familiar first, exploration second.** “Oatmeal” is what customers understand; lunch grains and grits can follow after traction.
- **Menu simplicity:** one prepared plain oatmeal base; different flavors assembled from preportioned toppings; **no eggs** in the initial breakfast offering.
- **One operator, plug-in electrical supply, predictable hours** are the default assumptions.
- **Business quality matters more than volume:** these documents show **gross sales**, not profit. Ingredients, packaging, fees, fuel, maintenance, payment processing, spoilage, taxes, permitting, and the operator's time are not zero simply because no employee wages are assumed.
- **Do not mistake brainstorms for legally cleared decisions:** trademark, parcel-specific permits, safe food preparation, and vehicle towing all require confirmation.
