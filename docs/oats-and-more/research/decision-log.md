## Why the idea has evolved

1. **Familiar product first:** oatmeal is easy for customers to recognize; “etc.” promises interesting variations without requiring someone to understand unfamiliar grains.
2. **Habits beat random traffic:** repeatable office-worker demand at a fixed time has better validation value than roaming events or hoping for passersby.
3. **Plug-in station beats battery complexity:** once you have a fixed approved host with electricity, moving the selling station electrically becomes an avoidable design constraint.
4. **Volume changes the vehicle:** three small soup kettles are plausible for 30 bowls, but 50–60 bowls points toward larger hot-hold vessels and an easier fixed setup.
5. **Tow capacity is binding:** a beautiful small-house enclosed trailer can cost and weigh more than the 2017 Rogue can safely tow. A portable station or ultralight trailer is the current candidate until equipment is weighed or towing arrangements change.

## Possibilities intentionally **not decided**

- Whether a regular **cargo e-bike**, electric vending trike, a folding kiosk **inside the Rogue**, or a **lightweight utility trailer** is selected.
- Whether food preparation occurs at a licensed commissary, an approved host kitchen, or another CDH-approved facility.
- Which exact lot/building will host the operation; whether the host charges a fee or contributes electricity.
- Whether the business serves 2, 3, 4, or 5 mornings each week.
- Serving size, actual final recipe, and topping/preparation workflow.
- Exact pricing, packaging, payment flow, trailer hardware, and whether preorders are needed.
- The legal availability and final selection of **Oats, Etc.**, alternate names, and taglines.
- Whether the current domain and GitHub Pages URL will be migrated; no change needed for the existing Worker/D1 to keep collecting votes.

## Decision rule

Only move from sketch to purchase after answering: **Where will we serve? How many real buyers are there? What can CDH and the City approve? Can the vehicle safely carry or tow the compliant configuration?**
