## Decision-sensitive gaps

1. **Choose the buying occasion.** A written comparison of employer-funded delivery and individual walk-up service should identify the buyer, current alternative, promise, access and evidence for each. Without it, total orders and acquisition costs have an unstable meaning.
2. **Check the economic cushion of that exact service.** The O3 illustrative 30-bowl/eight-cocoa employer order left $59.14 before operator pay and omitted costs. It returned $16.90/hour at 3.5 hours and $13.14/hour at 4.5 hours. These historical scenario outputs justify examining complete labor and price. They establish no achieved earnings.
3. **Define the next learning step.** Decide which material uncertainty is worth resolving and how. Keep interviews, supplier contact and testing as explicit choices. Use the run record when a round is scheduled.
