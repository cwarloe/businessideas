# Fudge — Boise business exploration

**Started:** November 15, 2026. **Stage:** Brainstorming and demand validation. No name, recipe, sales channel or launch commitment selected.

A separate business project alongside [Oats, Etc.](../oats-and-more/README.md), following the same concept → local research → economics → pilot structure. Fudge is the product category; this is not an extension of the oatmeal brand.

## Start here

**Working recommendation:** Test a small, preorder-based fudge gift business before considering a shop, trailer or large market commitment. Compare an approachable everyday treat with a gift-ready flight; learn which occasion actually makes people buy.

Boise is not an empty fudge market. Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory in Meridian explicitly advertises store-made fudge; Lee’s is an established Boise confectioner with documented fudge production; Weiser Classic Candy sells fudge online. Being homemade or offering an assortment will not by itself establish a distinctive position. These findings support testing a sharper offer, not a conclusion that demand is saturated.

## What is decided

Only the research scope: explore fudge in Boise and the Treasure Valley. Recommendations in this folder are proposals. All proposed names, prices, flavors, budgets and success thresholds remain unvalidated. No businesses have been contacted, orders accepted or events booked.

## Questions that most affect the direction

1. Is the appeal making fudge yourself, building a sales business with a production partner, or either?
2. Do you imagine an occasional seasonal side business or a recurring weekly operation?
3. Would you rather sell affordable personal treats, gift boxes, or larger office orders?

The first pilot can stay small while these preferences develop. Existing Oats, Etc. assets and its live voting integration are unchanged.
