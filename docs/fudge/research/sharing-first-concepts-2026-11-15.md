# Sharing-first shortlist and website studio

**Updated:** November 15, 2026. **User preference:** Develop Fudge Social, Pass the Fudge and Fudge for Friends around sharing. The other concepts remain research history, not the current priority. The personal-name concept remains excluded.

## Try the studio

Open [the sample website studio](../index.html). It offers three starting combinations, three independently selectable visual styles and four sharing occasions. Names, looks and occasions can be mixed into 36 combinations, with URL state, device-local favorites and a sample box-size explorer.

No orders, votes, reservations, payments or messages are collected. Illustrative box prices are $16 for 8 oz and $28 for 16 oz. These are pricing hypotheses, not validated offers. The photo is AI-generated exploration imagery; recipes, cut sizes, ingredients and handling remain unconfirmed. Optional notes are held only in the current page and are not stored or transmitted.

## Three starting combinations

| Name | Core promise | Starting look | Lead occasion | Headline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fudge Social | Shared enjoyment and friendly discovery | Coral/teal, bold type, split hero, flavor numbers | Game night | Pick a flavor. Defend your favorite. |
| Pass the Fudge | The action of sharing, built into the name | Rust/forest mercantile, photographic hero, warm table mood | Any get-together | Open the box. Pass it around. |
| Fudge for Friends | A personal contribution to time together | Pine/cream, editorial serif, image-first layout | Dinner with friends | You bring the fudge. |

The name, look and reason to buy are separate choices. For example, Fudge Social can use pine/cream styling and a coffee-and-conversation message. Changing the occasion changes the hero copy, explanation, box framing and closing campaign copy. Changing a name does not reset the selected look or occasion. Choosing a starting combination intentionally resets all three.

## Four campaigns to test

### Game night: flavor discovery

- **Buyer:** Someone contributing a treat to a gathering.
- **Message:** “Pick a flavor. Defend your favorite.”
- **Offer hypothesis:** Four flavors, already cut, in one sharing box. A taste-and-rank card is optional, not required to enjoy the product.
- **Sample post:** “Your strategy can wait. Your favorite fudge flavor cannot. Bring four flavors to game night and let the friendly debate begin.”
- **Evidence:** Full-price sampler purchases and repeat orders for this occasion. Votes alone are not demand.

### Dinner with friends: bring the good part

- **Buyer:** A guest who wants something easy and enjoyable to bring.
- **Message:** “You bring the fudge. They’ll make room.”
- **Offer hypothesis:** Ready-to-share box with an optional note for the host. A real site must show actual pickup windows before promising convenience.
- **Sample post:** “They invited you over. You brought something worth passing around. A little chocolate, a few favorites, and no dessert assembly.”
- **Evidence:** Paid purchases for a specific upcoming dinner and actual time spent packing/handoff.

### Coffee and conversation: a proper catch-up

- **Buyer:** A friend planning a visit or inviting someone over.
- **Message:** “Put the coffee on. Pass the fudge.”
- **Offer hypothesis:** The same sharing box, framed as a contribution to a visit. Test a smaller format only after understanding the existing box economics.
- **Sample post:** “Two cups. A few pieces of fudge. A conversation that deserves more than a quick hello.”
- **Evidence:** Purchases outside gift holidays; repeat buying as a casual treat.

### Any get-together: ordinary hospitality

- **Buyer:** Someone wanting a simple reason to linger with friends.
- **Message:** “You don’t need a special occasion. Just good company.”
- **Offer hypothesis:** A dependable, plainly priced box for the middle of the table.
- **Sample post:** “The plans don’t have to be elaborate. Pull up a chair, open the box, and pass something good around.”
- **Evidence:** Nonholiday purchases without discounting, along with contribution per owner hour.
