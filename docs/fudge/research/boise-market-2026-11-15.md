There are confirmed regional fudge offerings and established confectionery competitors. This search does not establish market size, revenue, sales volume, market share or unmet demand. Online visibility is especially incomplete for home producers and seasonal booths. “Not found” does not mean absent or closed.

### Published price anchor

Weiser’s retrieved offer is **$29.99 / 24 oz = about $1.25/oz or $19.99/lb**, before any applicable shipping and tax. The listing describes six pieces and multiple collections [S6]. Record the retrieval date when comparing later. A proposed 8 oz box at $14–$18 would be $28–$36/lb, a material premium requiring evidence that packaging, taste and convenience earn it. Do not compare unequal package weights or treat a regional online price as a Boise market average.

## Plausible openings — hypotheses, not discovered gaps

- **Easy small gifts:** a clearly priced box for a host, coworker or thank-you occasion, with predictable pickup.
- **Office gifting:** one order, individually labeled boxes and a single local delivery window. Existing confectioners already serve gifts; the hypothesis is easier ordering and fulfillment for a specific buyer.
- **Tasting flights:** four approachable flavors, small portions and a simple flavor card. Assortments alone are not novel; test whether presentation and portion size matter.
- **Seasonal drops:** limited production with a preorder cutoff. This could fit limited weekly availability, but holiday success would not establish year-round demand.

Potential substitutes include chocolates, cookies, brownies, bakery boxes and other local gifts. Interview customers about their last actual purchase and alternative, not only whether they like fudge.

## Places to investigate selling

**Capital City Public Market:** official vendor application and specialty-food requirements are published [S10]. Ask about category openings, production requirements, sampling, fees, insurance and next available dates. No opening or acceptance is established here.

**Boise Farmers Market:** publishes a vendor-selection process [S11]. Its 2026 winter page lists Saturday markets through December 19 at Idaho Botanical Garden [S12]. This is evidence of an event, not an available booth. Ask whether fudge fits its agriculture-focused selection and when the next application window opens.

Other prospect categories: employer-hosted pop-ups, independent coffee shops, realtors and event planners. These are brainstorming targets, not verified interested partners. Coffee-shop resale raises different requirements from a producer selling directly at a host location.

## Field-research shopping list

Visit RMCF Meridian, Lee’s and Cravin’s Meridian. Confirm fudge availability, photograph prices only with permission where needed, buy comparable weights, and record texture, sweetness, packaging, flavor range, price/oz and gift ordering. Optional: buy Weiser’s assortment. Set a research spending cap before purchasing; nothing has been bought.
