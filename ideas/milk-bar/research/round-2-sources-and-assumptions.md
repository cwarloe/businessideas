## Model assumptions and replacement evidence

| Input | Reference | Downside or open question | Replace with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator objective | No owner-specified earnings hurdle or startup cap. $16.46/hour is an optional market comparison. | A handoff could mean independent ownership or paid employment. | Record who owns inventory, takes payment, supplies labor and bears loss. Enter payroll burden for employees. |
| Paid demand | 40 retail orders in two hours, 80 per six-hour shop day, 30 flights for a private booking. | 20 in two hours, 40 shop orders, 20 booked flights. | Same-site paid volume by time block or a binding event headcount/price. No observed demand. |
| Retail mix | Plain 10%, signatures 65%, flights 25%. Full SKU weights on Menu. | 20%, 30%, 50%. | Actual itemized tickets, including group-flight buyers. |
| Recipe and ticket | 10 oz plain base or 10 oz plus 0.75 oz flavor in a 12 oz cup. Flight has four 1.5 oz pours. $3.50/$4 plain, $6 signatures/flight, pre-tax. | No demonstrated local willingness to pay for this menu. | Weighed recipe, consumer comparison and paid tickets. |
| Production yield | 90% milk and 95% flavor usable. Common yield simplifies model. | 70% milk, 95% flavor. Small-volume rice and soy could be substantially worse. | Per base and lot, weigh received/opened/discarded stock. Price reorder increments and shelf life. |
| Labor and throughput | Single station, 40–120 seconds/item, 80% usable time. Host two service hours plus two other hours. Public two plus four. Shop six plus three. Private 1.5 plus five. | Untimed. Travel and selling/booking effort can run longer. Four-hour market sensitivity models posted selling hours. | Include shopping, outreach, prep, loading, serving, sanitation, reconciliation and both people's time. |
| Cash processing | One card ticket per retail item. | Dedicated unit | $1,500/mo base rent for 1,000 sq ft in a triple-net listing. | No verification of suitable food use, utilities, tenant improvements, insurance or deposits. | Landlord, city, district, equipment and installation quotes. |

## Unresolved evidence at handoff

1. CDH received the preliminary classification inquiry November 18. Attach its eventual response, the operator identity, location diagram and exact menu before relying on a path. Verify municipal use and selling requirements for the chosen address.
2. Secure a single named partner's written fee/share, kitchen, staffing, storage and seller responsibilities. No host was contacted or booked. Identify whether customers will be present during the intended hours and who buys a private event.
3. Match labels and UPCs, especially the rice product. Verify freight, cold delivery, returns and the landed cost of smaller local packs. The individual retail and case benchmarks do not form a supplier offer.
4. Obtain actual cleaning, travel, utilities, insurance, training, equipment, deposits and wage burden. Enter the quote and its date once. Avoid counting kitchen time or operator wages twice.
5. Quality at service temperature, timed simultaneous orders, yield by base and return demand remain unmeasured. No sampling, sales, or market applications took place.
