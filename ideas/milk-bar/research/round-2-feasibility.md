# Milk Bar Round 2 · Boise product, location and operating feasibility

November 18, 2026 · Desktop feasibility study and operator handoff · All amounts USD, before income tax

**Pursue a recurring counter inside an existing Boise food business only if the partner can supply both an equipped station and customers at modest cost.** The strongest named retail lead is an existing vendor at The Warehouse Food Hall. Flava Kitchen is a stronger lead for a prepaid tasting where the group buyer covers the room. Boise Farmers Market is a weak first choice at its published fees. Hold a dedicated lease. There is no host agreement, paid demand evidence or agency decision on this operation. [L02] [P12] [P14]

At 40 orders, a four-hour host session leaves **at most $154.73** for the operator and all unquoted costs. At 20 orders with more flights and more spoilage it leaves **$69.34**. If four hours of kitchen access cost $120, those amounts fall to **$34.73 and a $50.66 loss**. A young operator inheriting this concept would benefit more from a host that brings paying customers and shares facilities than from an inexpensive carton of milk. The owner specified no minimum income or capital limit.

If the opened stock cannot be used within its safe life, move rice to prebooked flights or source a smaller pack. |

Pacific's general opened guidance and the rice label reference 7–10 refrigerated days. CDH's ready-to-eat time/temperature controlled food guidance limits qualifying products to seven days at 41°F or colder, counting opening/preparation as day one. Use the earlier applicable limit and have CDH classify the specific opened products and intended holding periods. Preserve the lot and opening/discard dates on every carton. Use pasteurized dairy only. Commercial sauce guidance describes four weeks after opening for best quality. Read each bottle's actual instructions. Aseptic sealed case life does not establish the shelf life of an open carton. [G03] [G04] [T03] [T04] [T05] [T07]

**Station flow.** Receive and log supplier, lot, condition and temperature. Move chilled goods directly into approved refrigeration. For an off-site event, prechill product, use an insulated carrier with a cold source and a calibrated probe, log departure, arrival and service temperatures, and follow the district's disposition procedure for excursions. Set cold storage, measured flavor pumps and labeled pitchers behind the counter. Keep payment and guest tasting apart from pouring. Set aside dirty utensils for wash, rinse, sanitize and air dry. Provide handwashing, potable water, sanitary wastewater disposal and adequate cold capacity for the opened cartons during the busiest hour. A cooler purchase alone proves no cold-holding performance. [G03] [G05] [G06] [P18] [P19]

**Allergens and recipe scope.** Show the precise ingredient list for each base and flavor. Identify milk and soy explicitly, retain each lot's packaging, and describe shared-workspace exposure. Use distinct labeled pitchers, jiggers and stirrers per base, clean the work surface, wash hands and fully clean reusable equipment between incompatible orders. Four simultaneous flight pours multiply opportunities for mix-ups. If staff cannot resolve an allergy question from current labels and the actual station, explain that limitation before taking payment. Defer nut bases and hot drinks. Each expansion adds storage, cross-contact or temperature/texture decisions. No tasting, heating or stress test of these recipes has been conducted. [T01] [T02] [T05] [T06]

The flight contributes about $2.28 per active station minute versus $4.19 for the strawberry signature. A line of flight buyers can reduce the number of later drink orders served. These estimates need timed observation. A paid employee working alongside the owner adds wages even if the shared pour station stays the bottleneck.

**The actual Boise market day lasts four selling hours.** With four additional work hours, $219.92 of known costs and unchanged *hourly* demand, the reference case sells 80 orders and leaves $12.75/hour. The downside sells 40 and loses $8.60/hour. At unchanged *total* orders of 40/20, the eight-hour ceilings are −$7.37/−$18.04 per hour. Earning the Boise wage comparison takes at least 88/94 orders respectively across the four-hour market. Capacity is 144/129 under the assumed mix and pace. The amount of paid traffic needed, eligibility of the actual menu and the full day of operator time make this different from a two-hour indoor host counter. [P14] [P20]

**Handoff to another operator.** An independent operator's pre-tax return is cash remaining after all operating bills divided by *all* hours. No compensation target was imposed. The May 2025 Boise metro mean of $16.46 for food preparation and serving occupations is a comparison point. Four hours at that rate use $65.84, leaving **$88.89** in the 40-order host case or **$3.50** in the 20-order case for every remaining expense. At a $120 Flava fee and reference mix, roughly 48 orders are needed merely to cover the fee, allocated insurance and four hours at this comparison wage. If Charles employs the operator, enter actual wages and employer payroll costs, pay for hours worked and value Charles's separate supervision/admin time. Residual business profit belongs to whoever bears the risk under the arrangement. If the proposed employee is under 18, verify age-specific hours, tasks and equipment rules before assigning work. CDH publishes different facilities guidance for fixed and temporary operations. A fixed counter may need permanently installed sinks and an approved three-compartment sink or commercial dishwasher. CDH's temporary manual describes a handwash station with a continuous flow of warm potable water, soap, towels and a catch basin, plus a separately approved means to wash, rinse and sanitize utensils. Keep an unobstructed path to handwashing. Use the district's plan review and the facility's exact fixtures to determine what is acceptable. Idaho's home-production law changed March 20, 2026. This proposed operation uses a commercial food-service site. The home-production guidance supplies no affirmative clearance for it. [G01] [G03] [G05] [G06] [G11]

In Boise, ask the City Clerk to classify the actual seller and address under the vendor or applicable food/beverage rules. Planning and Development Services handles use and occupancy. The city distinguishes an advertised public-property event from a private-property event for its special-events application. Other permits and property-owner consent can still apply. Determine Idaho seller/event tax registration and who collects tax. All shortlisted locations above are in Ada County. If the concept moves to Nampa or Caldwell, take the plan to Southwest District Health and that city before carrying over any Boise determination. [G07] [G08] [G09] [G10] [G13] [G14] [G16]

## Decision and outstanding evidence

1. **Lead with the partner economics in discussions.** Verify available customer hours and the complete charge for host facilities, staffing and settlement. At 20 orders, even a $5 extra charge consumes more than the $3.50 remaining after the local wage comparison. At 40, it is worth pricing the arrangement. A partner cannot supply low or free labor indefinitely without somebody bearing the cost.
2. **Keep the private tasting as a separate business model.** The reference $283.59 group invoice floor is about $9.45 per attendee before remaining costs. Someone must actually buy the whole booking at a sufficient price. General enthusiasm for sampling gives no support for that invoice.
3. **Keep both customer appeals open.** Fast signatures and the four-base tasting solve different customer problems. Serving a flight may be commercially sound at a quiet event while displacing higher-contribution drinks during a retail rush. Repeat full-price purchases, selection by base and flavors that taste good cold remain unresolved.
4. **Hold the market and storefront.** Market attendance/fees, local-ingredient eligibility and labor erode the first option. The shop adds fixed commitments ahead of evidence of differentiated demand. Revisit only when observed demand, signed site costs, food-safety classification and operator return support the added exposure.
5. **Handoff sequence.** Retain the CDH response when it arrives. Obtain a written host proposal and the matching city determination, enter landed supplier and equipment quotes, then rerun both workbook cases using actual operator hours. A separate decision would be needed for field validation. No sales, taste tests, purchases, lease or website were part of this round.
