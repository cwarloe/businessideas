## Decision-sensitive gaps

1. **Separate the purchase occasions.** A cold cup, a warm cup, a household container and a recurring delivery can solve different jobs. Write the alternatives side by side before choosing one to develop.
2. **Model the relevant transaction.** M2's forty hypothetical host orders leave an upper bound of $154.73 before unresolved charges, and a $120 kitchen example cuts that to $34.73. Those historical scenario ceilings show sensitivity to facilities. Bottle resale and a route need their own margin, order density and time calculations.
3. **Select useful evidence at the owner's pace.** Existing research and reviews can inform the offer comparison. Buyer behavior remains a gap until direct or test evidence is obtained. Outreach readiness is a choice.
