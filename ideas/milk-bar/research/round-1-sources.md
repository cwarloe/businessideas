## Search coverage, negative findings and unresolved conflicts

- Local searches combined Boise, Meridian, Nampa and Treasure Valley with milk flight, milk tasting, fresh strawberry milk, plant milk, dairy farm store, boba, milkshake, smoothie, dessert flight, grocery and event vendors. Search results also identified Eagle, Kuna and Melba alternatives. Verified offers by product and address where possible. No exhaustive census or on-site check was performed.
- Analog searches included US fair concessions and farm tours, UK cereal cafés and dairy vending, Japanese milk counters and Japanese-language milk-comparison menus, Philippine fresh-milk drinks and plant-milk promotions. Searches for café closure, lease nonrenewal, subsidy and online pivots supplied counterexamples.
- **Not found:** Boise paid four-base plain flight menu, receipts for such a product, customer cohort or representative complaint data, an exclusive local concept, a confirmed host, accepted event quote or operator-hour economics. An unindexed stall or different menu term can evade a search.
- **Channel conflicts:** L01's indexed and readable copies vary in price visibility. L02's other rotating flavor varies by snapshot. L08 is a timed association offer. A06 is a booking listing on a different platform from the link visible in A05. A10 is a dynamic operator menu whose indexed text matches the third-party transcription in A11. Use each only for its labeled claim.
- **Status conflicts:** A19 still describes visiting the cafés. A17 and dated A18 support a closure and online pivot. A16 concerns one Milk Bar lease while A15 lists other locations. A13's event-program content refers to 2026 despite a site header advertising 2027. G02 has seasonal reopening language without confirming current availability.
- **Unsupported assertions excluded:** claimed café or flight margins, market share, Boise willingness to pay at foreign prices, brand-wide failure, raw demand from fair attendance, health outcomes, permitting eligibility and legal name clearance. No cost quotes, field interviews or live pilot results were collected.
