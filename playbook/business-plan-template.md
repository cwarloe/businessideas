# Working business plan template

Copy this file to your idea folder as `business-plan.md`. Use it after the first market and economics pass. Fill what the evidence supports, label assumptions and leave open questions visible. Update it after each test. Keep the research, cost model and test records linked so you can trace decisions back to the evidence.
