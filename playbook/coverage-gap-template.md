## Prioritized gap decisions

Name the two or three uncertainties most likely to change the decision. Explain their consequence and cost/time to resolve, then choose one suitable next learning step. Owner and due date remain OPEN until assigned. Identify evidence that would count against the hypothesis. Preserve failed or inconclusive outcomes.
