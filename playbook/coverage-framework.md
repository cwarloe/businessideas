# Business idea coverage and evidence framework

Reviewed November 18, 2026. Use this alongside the [playbook](../BUSINESS_IDEA_PLAYBOOK.md). It turns the consultant's process recommendations and selected published course topics into a reusable coverage check. The [source crosswalk](course-and-consultant-alignment.md) identifies which elements come from a course, SBA guidance or our practical adaptation.

## Sequence and depth

| Depth | Decision being supported | Expected work | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen | Is this idea worth a closer look? | Brief, alternative offers, customer occasions, competitor/substitute scan, rough economics and fatal-constraint check | Continue assessment, revise or park, with major unknowns visible. |
| Assess | Which specific business model deserves validation? | Reasoned segment/offer/channel choice, research method, operating process, cost/capacity model, cash needs, scenarios and prioritized gaps | A working plan and a proportionate next learning step. |
| Validate | Does behavior and actual delivery support a commitment? | Suitable buyer evidence, measured price/volume/cost/time, operational permissions for the activity, small test and opportunity to repeat | Continue, revise, park or inconclusive, with the next commitment bounded. |
| Formalize | Can we defend the plan for launch, handoff, coursework or funding? | Integrated plan, responsibilities, milestones, financing, financial forecasts and evidence appendix tailored to the audience | A decision supported at the depth the recipient or commitment requires. |

Discovery, rough economics and feasibility overlap from the screen onward. Refine the customer and offer before optimizing promotion. Start the working plan early and revise it throughout. Finalize the executive recommendation after reconciling the evidence. A small experiment can proceed with explicitly deferred topics that do not affect it. Detail should increase with the risk and size of the commitment.

Learning methods can include existing evidence, public reviews, observation, an experience walkthrough, interviews, quotations and paid tests. Select the method that can answer the question and fits the founder's readiness. Record its limits. Each case below is an assessment example. External outreach is deferred under the owner's current instruction.

## Coverage areas

Use the IDs in a [copyable gap register](coverage-gap-template.md). The evidence listed is a way to substantiate an answer at the relevant depth. Its presence does not automatically establish demand or profitability.

| ID | Topic and governing question | Screen and assessment output | What stronger evidence adds | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Purpose and founder/operator fit. What outcome and constraints govern the idea? | Motivation, available resources, operator, decision, limits and open preferences | Accepted roles, chosen return requirement, funding limits | Consultant, SBA |
| A02 | Customer and purchase occasion. Who pays, uses, approves and grants access? | Candidate segments, recent behavior or stated evidence gap, decision process | Direct observations, actual purchase records and qualified buyer accounts | MIT entrepreneurship/marketing |
| A03 | Market and context. How much relevant demand can we reach? | Industry/local context, seasonality, reachable population, conversion and frequency assumptions | Observed audience, validated access and defensible demand estimate | MIT entrepreneurship/marketing |
| A04 | Alternatives and advantage. Why switch or stay? | Direct competitors, substitutes, DIY/current habit, positioning and reasons to doubt differentiation | Buyer comparisons and behavioral evidence of preference | MIT entrepreneurship/marketing |
| A05 | Offer and experience. What exactly will be bought and delivered? | Versions, portions/features, price hypothesis, purchase-to-use journey | Quality, usability, willingness to pay and delivery observations | MIT entrepreneurship, SBA |
| A06 | Marketing, sales and retention. How does a stranger become a repeat buyer? | Coordinated product/price/place/promotion, sales steps, access, acquisition cash/time and reorder path | Conversion by channel, acquisition payback and repeat contribution | MIT entrepreneurship/marketing |
| A07 | Research quality. How trustworthy is the conclusion? | Question, sources/dates, sampling/recruitment, contradictions, evidence limits | Anonymized observations, counts, test comparisons and uncertainty | Georgia Tech, consultant |
| A08 | Operations and permissions. Can we deliver the promise? | Suppliers, workflow, equipment, bottleneck/capacity and relevant permissions | Actual terms, process rehearsal, appropriate approvals and real failures | Consultant, SBA |
| A09 | Economics and cash. Can it pay and remain solvent? | Contribution, overhead, all labor, startup/working cash, downside, timing and cushion | Landed costs, actual sales/work/waste, cash movements and sensitivity | MIT entrepreneurship, SBA, consultant |
| A10 | Organization and resources. Who owns, runs, funds and decides? | Ownership/roles, skills gaps, dependencies, tentative funding/use | Accepted responsibilities, staffing costs and available resources | MIT entrepreneurship, SBA |
| A11 | Validation and decisions. What result would change the next commitment? | Ranked uncertainty, test method, thresholds, cap, disconfirming evidence and review trigger | Executed results, repeat opportunity and resulting decision | Consultant |
| A12 | Integrated plan and communication. Do the claims fit together? | Short working plan, summary, milestones and links to evidence | Forecast reconciliation, defensible presentation, revision history and handoff | MIT entrepreneurship/marketing, SBA |

A forecast should reconcile reachable audience → exposure → paid buyers → orders/frequency → revenue → capacity/cost → cash and operator return. Explain estimates, mismatched time periods and evidence gaps at each link.

## Operating literacy before launch

A08 includes daily orders, stock, quality and customer issue handling. A09 includes the ability to record and reconcile transactions, distinguish earned sales from cash collected, read basic statements, forecast obligations and assess owner return. A10 assigns spending, refund, reconciliation and review responsibility. A12 links actual operating reviews back to the plan.

If the owner has never operated a business, record the learning gap explicitly. Use the [beginner guide](running-the-business.md), [practice month](practice-business-month.md) and [review template](operating-review-template.md) while still at Screen or Assess depth. Understanding these relationships can be practiced before external engagement. Demonstrating the skill is a separate outcome from validating the product's demand.

## How to record a gap accurately

Track **coverage** separately from **evidence**.

- Coverage uses MISSING, PARTIAL, DRAFTED or REVIEWED. REVIEWED means the question has been addressed and checked for the chosen depth.
- Evidence uses ASSUMED, SOURCE-SUPPORTED, FIRSTHAND or OBSERVED-TEST. Mixes are allowed with explanation. A supplier's receipt is firsthand cost evidence, while demand could remain assumed.
- DEFERRED or NOT-APPLICABLE describes timing or relevance and needs a reason and revisit trigger.
- Record the decision affected, required depth, evidence needed, candidate method, responsible role and priority. Use OPEN when the person or date has not been assigned.
- Priority uses NOW if it changes the current decision, BEFORE-TEST for the proposed activity, BEFORE-INVESTMENT for a larger commitment, and LATER for a deferred topic.
- Close a gap only against its stated evidence requirement. Preserve negative findings and the source/version that changed the decision. A completed table can still recommend parking the idea.
