# Idaho legal requirements · applicability and evidence checklist

Reviewed November 18, 2026. Use this with the [Idaho operations guide](idaho-startup-and-operations.md) and [obligations register](idaho-obligations-template.md). Idaho is the current example jurisdiction. The exact duties depend on the activity, entity, operator, location, employees and transactions. This source-linked planning checklist identifies questions for verification. Completion requires evidence about the actual business.

**Current examples** — Proposed Oats and Milk Bar ventures in Boise/Ada County. Ownership/entity, seller, site and staffing remain open. Their existing research and a preliminary food-safety inquiry establish no permission to begin operating.

## Identify the basis of each requirement

Use LAW/REGULATION for an applicable legal duty, CONTRACT for an actual lease/host/supplier/insurance condition, and MANAGEMENT-PRACTICE for a recommended control. A host can require evidence of insurance by contract. Employment law can independently require workers' compensation. Record the basis rather than treating every useful precaution as a universal statutory rule.

## Checklist by trigger

| Topic | Trigger and requirement to resolve | Evidence to retain | Official basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, entity and business identity | Before trading under the chosen identity, determine Secretary of State registration and any qualifying full-personal-name sole-proprietor exception. Check the chosen legal structure and signatory authority. | Registration/entity record or documented exception, ownership/authority | L01, L02 |
| Entity maintenance | If the chosen entity requires a registered agent or annual report, maintain current information and file on its actual cycle. | SOSBiz record, agent details, filing confirmation and due date | L02 |
| Professional/activity licensing | If the activity is regulated, obtain its required license. Idaho's general registration guidance identifies no statewide general business license. Use the Business Wizard and responsible regulator. | Agency/activity determination, license and renewal | L03 |
| Local use and site | The actual municipality, parcel, venue and selling method determine local licensing, zoning, home occupation, occupancy/building/fire or vending permissions. | Local determination, approvals and property/host agreement as applicable | L03 |
| Food operation | Where the proposed food activity is regulated, resolve district review, licensure, inspection and process requirements before service. Identify the responsible operator and source/prep/storage/transport/service process. | Written classification, approved plan/menu/site, license/inspection and operating records | L04 |
| Tax registration and sales/use tax | Establish applicable accounts or a verified exemption. Determine taxable sales/fees, purchasing/use tax and filing obligations for the actual seller. | Account notice, exemption basis, invoices, records, returns and confirmations | L05, L06 |
| Federal/state income and employment taxes | Entity and owner/employment facts determine tax IDs, returns, payments and payroll reporting. | Actual accountant/agency setup, schedules, records and returns | L05 and the Idaho guide's IRS/Tax Commission sources |
| Employment classification and pay | Before using paid help, determine employment status under the applicable rules. Apply wage, hours/overtime, payday, deduction and recordkeeping duties. Check youth restrictions if relevant. | Classification basis, pay notice, time/wage records and payroll calendar | L07 |
| Hiring administration | If hiring employees, resolve eligibility verification, required notices, withholding and unemployment setup. Idaho generally requires new-hire reporting within 20 days. | Actual onboarding forms, reports, notices and account confirmations | L08, L09 |
| Workers' compensation | Employers generally need coverage before hiring the first employee unless a specific exemption applies. Verify the proposed worker and entity. | Policy effective before covered work, or documented exemption basis | L10 |
| Workplace safety | Covered employers must meet applicable workplace-safety duties. Determine coverage and relevant hazards/equipment practices. | Training, relevant procedures, incident/reporting arrangements and required records | L11 |
| Accessible public service | Where the business is a public accommodation, assess applicable ADA access, policies and effective-communication duties for the premises/service. | Site/service assessment, allocated responsibilities and necessary actions | L12 |
| Honest sales and customer dealings | Advertising, price/ingredient claims, subscriptions, deposits and cancellation/refund communications must comply with applicable consumer law. | Actual offer, terms shown to customers, proof for claims and complaint/refund records | L13 |
| Personal information and incidents | If the operation maintains covered personal information or experiences a breach, determine applicable Idaho data-breach duties. | Data inventory, incident assessment and required notifications, stored privately | L14 |
| Equipment, assets and business changes | Business property reporting, exemption, entity/address/ownership changes or closure may create further duties. | Assessor/account/entity determinations and confirmations | Idaho guide I05/I16 and L02 |

The law and contract categories can overlap. For a rented kitchen, host consent covers agreed access. The operator also needs the applicable regulatory path. A contractor label, small revenue amount or borrowed location alone does not settle applicability.

## Application to Oats and Milk Bar

| Proposed activity | Legal questions still open |
|---|---|
| Oats cooked in a commercial kitchen and served or delivered to an office | Who is the operator/seller, what food process and site are approved, which invoice/tax treatment applies, and who is responsible for food safety, transport, customer terms and staff? |
| Milk poured cold or warmed at a stand | Which source, process, station, local vending/use and health approvals apply? What claims, ingredient/allergen information and service arrangements are required? |
| Supplier-sealed milk pickup or delivery | Which retail/resale and food-handling path applies to sealed product, storage and route? What tax registration/exemption and delivery terms apply? |
| Either venture operated by another person | Is that person an owner, employee or properly classified independent business? Who signs contracts, employs staff, collects/remits tax, handles refunds and keeps records? |

Use the actual product/process to investigate any food-law exception, labeling/manufacturing issue or supplier limitation. For home preparation, consult current Idaho DHW guidance and the relevant regulator before choosing that path. The previous cold-counter inquiry concerns a narrower hypothetical process.

## Before the first relevant commitment

1. Describe the entity, seller/operator, product, address, prep/service/delivery and staffing in plain language.
2. For each applicable row, record the actual rule/authority, applicability basis, date checked, responsible person, trigger/due date and evidence needed.
3. Verify material unresolved items with the responsible agency or an Idaho attorney/accountant. Match verification to the activity being planned.
4. Review contracts before signing. Document permitted use, responsibility for approvals, fees, insurance, storage, sanitation, refunds and termination as relevant.
5. Put ongoing duties into the operating calendar. Retain confirmations and update them when the model, location or personnel change.

Planning notes can stay open during idea assessment. A commitment to operate needs the applicable obligations resolved before their trigger. This document creates no registration, tax election, contract, policy or approval.
