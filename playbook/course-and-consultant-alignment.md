## Consultant recommendations and practical adaptations

The independent AI consultant reviewed the repository process and examples. It endorsed early economics, all operator time, negative findings and bounded testing. It recommended earlier customer learning alongside constraint checks, deliberate segment choice, acquisition/retention economics, cash timing, executable tests and a clear next decision.

Our Screen → Assess → Validate → Formalize depths, twelve coverage IDs, evidence labels, gap priorities, operating cushion, timeboxes and test-run record are practical adaptations. The published sources do not prescribe those exact labels, universal interview counts, or our hypothetical numeric thresholds. Founder readiness governs whether a learning step involves observation, a conversation or an external request. The attempted outreach sequence is deferred after owner feedback.

## Formal submission checklist when relevant

Use this to interpret an actual assignment or funding request. Record the requirement, recipient, evidence depth and location in the plan. Include an executive recommendation, market/customer research, justified strategy and marketing mix, operations and management, economics/cash/funding, milestones, and evidence appendix. A presentation should explain why the recommendation follows and what would overturn it.

For a larger commitment, reconcile monthly demand, revenue, staffing, operating costs and cash. Add balance-sheet, financing and longer-horizon forecasts where called for. Early assessments can leave these explicitly deferred with a trigger. Financial detail cannot remove uncertainty about buyer behavior.

Assess the quality of the work by its traceability, consistency, handling of contrary evidence and usefulness for the decision. No academic grade or universal school-compliance certification is implied.
