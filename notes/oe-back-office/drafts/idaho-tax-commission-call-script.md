# Call script: Idaho State Tax Commission

Use this after you know you may make a paid sale, and after you have at least a working idea of your entity (sole proprietor or LLC). Do not file a permit or a return from this call. See [register.md](../register.md) items TAX-01, TAX-02, and TAX-03.

**Published contact (opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session):** (208) 334-7660 or 1-800-972-7660. [Getting Tax Permits](https://tax.idaho.gov/online-services/business-registration/), [Who Needs a Seller's Permit](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/sales-use/permits/who-needs-a-sellers-permit/), [Temporary Seller's Permits](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/sales-use/permits/temporary-sellers-permits/), [Small Seller Exemption](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/sales-use/exemptions/sales-and-use-taxes-exemptions-for-individuals/small-seller-exemption/).

The Tax Commission says to register the business and any assumed business name with the Idaho Secretary of State before using the Idaho Business Registration application. Applying for a seller's permit is published as free. Online permits were described as arriving in 10 to 15 business days.

## Script

> Hello, my name is Charles Warloe. I may make a one-day retail sale of oatmeal breakfast bowls in the Boise area. I have not filed anything yet. I need to know which tax permit, if any, I need, and when I would have to collect and send in sales tax.
>
> I am an Idaho resident. I have not chosen a final business structure. I may operate as a sole proprietor, or I may form an LLC. The first event might be a single morning at a public event or at a private office.
>
> Questions:
> - For a one-day paid trial, do I need a temporary seller's permit, a regular seller's permit, or no permit?
> - If I stay a sole proprietor and this is a single small trial, do I meet the small seller exemption, or should I not rely on it?
> - I understand the published state sales tax rate is 6 percent, and that furnishing meals or drinks is a taxable example. Is that the rate I should collect on these bowls, and is any other local tax due for a food sale in Boise? I have read that the Greater Boise Auditorium District tax applies to short-term lodging, not meals. Can you confirm that for this sale?
> - If I need a temporary permit, how do I apply, how long is it valid, and when is the return due? I have read that some temporary returns are due 15 days after the permit expires or 15 days after the event. Is that still correct?
> - Do I need to finish a Secretary of State filing before I can apply?
> - Can you email a short confirmation or point me to the exact pages I should follow?
>
> My email is __________.

## Reminders (already opened on official pages, 2026-11-11)

- You do not qualify for the small seller exemption if you operate as an LLC, corporation, or partnership, if you are not an Idaho resident, if you maintain a permanent place of business, or if you believe you will exceed $5,000 in gross sales in the next calendar year.
- If you have a seller's permit, you must file a return even if you made no sales.
- Income tax is a separate question. Ask a tax professional about Idaho Form 40 and federal filing. Do not use this call as tax advice.
