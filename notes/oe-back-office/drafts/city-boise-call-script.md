# Call script: City of Boise Clerk, Planning, and ACHD

Use this after the Central District Health call, or in the same week if you already know the trial would be in Boise. If the site is in Meridian, Garden City, Eagle, or Nampa, do not use these Boise numbers. Call that city instead.

See [start-here.md](../start-here.md). A longer City and ACHD script is also in OE Kitchen's checklist in [pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4).

## Script B. City Clerk

**Published contact (opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session):** City Clerk, 150 N. Capitol Blvd, Boise, ID 83702. Phone (208) 972-8150. Hours: Monday through Wednesday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Thursday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., closed holidays, America/Boise. Sources: [Vendor License](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing/other/vendor-license/) and [Food and Beverage License](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing/food-and-alcohol-beverage/food-and-beverage-license/).

> Hello, my name is Charles Warloe. I am planning a possible one-day oatmeal breakfast trial in Boise and I need to know which city licenses apply. I am also working with Central District Health on the food license. I have not filed anything yet.
>
> The setup I am asking about is: [public event booth / portable table on a private office lot at (address) from 6:30 to 9:30 a.m. / catering drop-off]. I will not sell alcohol.
>
> Questions:
> - Do I need a Food and Beverage license, a Vendor license, a Temporary Merchant license, more than one, or none for this one day?
> - If Central District Health issues only a one-day Temporary Food Establishment license, do I still need a city license?
> - Are 6:30 a.m. sales allowed at this address? Any special downtown or sidewalk rules?
> - What documents do you need (Secretary of State registration, CDH permit, bond, fingerprinting, site map)?
> - What is the current fee, and how long does approval take?
> - Should I also talk to Planning, Parks, or ACHD for this site?
>
> My email is __________. Please confirm in writing if you can.

Published fees to confirm (fee schedule opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session, "all fees are subject to change"): Eating and Drinking / Food and Beverage $84.00; Vendor annual $148.00, semi-annual $74.00; Temporary Merchant $69.75; plus a $3.00 license processing fee listed separately. Vendor and temporary-merchant applications may also need a cash or surety bond of $1,000 per person or $5,000 per company. Temporary merchants may need in-person fingerprinting at City Hall. All business license applications must include a current Idaho Secretary of State registration that matches the name on the application. Source: the Clerk pages linked above, and [Licensing Fee Schedule](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing-fee-schedule/).

Do not apply until the Clerk tells you which license, if any, is required.

## Script D. ACHD (only if the sidewalk, curb, or right-of-way might be involved)

If the entire stand, the line, and the power cord stay on private pavement the owner controls, this is probably not your first call. If any part might sit on a sidewalk, curb, driveway apron, or planter, call.

**Published contact on the City vendor page (opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session):** Ada County Highway District, (208) 387-6100.

> Hello, my name is Charles Warloe. I am checking whether a possible one-morning food stand at (address) would sit in Ada County Highway District right-of-way. The property owner has given permission for private pavement, but I want to confirm where the public right-of-way begins, and whether a table, line, vehicle, or power cord would need an ACHD permit.
>
> I can email photos and a simple sketch. My email is __________. I am not asking you to approve a food license. I only need the right-of-way answer.

Do not plan to run an extension cord across a public sidewalk.
