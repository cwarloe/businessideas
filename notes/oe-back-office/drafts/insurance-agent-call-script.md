# Call script: insurance agent

Use this after Central District Health, a kitchen, a host, a market, or the City has told you what insurance they require, or if you want a quote once you know the trial type. Do not buy a policy until you have a written requirement and a quote you have reviewed. Premiums and limits are not confirmed in the register. This is not an insurance recommendation.

## Before you call

- Trial type and likely date: __________
- Site type (private office, public event, or not yet known): __________
- Will you cook in a rented commercial kitchen? __________
- Will you transport food in the 2017 Nissan Rogue? __________
- Written limits anyone has already asked for: __________
- Your current auto and home or renter insurer, if you will start with them: __________

## Script

> Hello, I am planning a possible one-day oatmeal breakfast trial in the Boise area. I need to know what insurance I should carry, and what it will cost, before I book a kitchen or an event.
>
> The operation would be a single operator selling or sampling hot oatmeal bowls, possibly at a private office or at a public market, and possibly cooking in a rented commercial kitchen and transporting food in my 2017 Nissan Rogue.
>
> Please tell me:
> - What general liability and product liability limits you recommend, and what a kitchen, city, or market would typically require. I will also ask any kitchen or host for their written requirement.
> - Whether product liability is included or separate, and whether it covers this menu, including nuts and any fruit topping.
> - Whether my personal auto policy covers business transport of food, or I need a commercial auto policy or an endorsement.
> - The cost and wording to add a kitchen, host, market, or city as additional insured, and to issue a certificate quickly.
> - Whether a one-day event rider is available.
> - If the City requires a $1,000 or $5,000 vendor bond, can you issue that, and is it separate from the liability policy?
>
> Please email a quote and a sample certificate requirement list. I have not set a date yet.

The City of Boise vendor page, opened 2026-11-11, says vendor and temporary-merchant applications may need a cash or surety bond of $1,000 per person or $5,000 per company, and that bonds can be obtained through your insurance company. That is a bond, not a liability policy. Confirm with the Clerk whether your license type needs it. Source: [Vendor License](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing/other/vendor-license/), VERIFIED-this-session.

Market or kitchen insurance limits reported in other OE notes were not re-opened from those third-party pages in this Back Office session. Ask each venue for their current written requirement.
