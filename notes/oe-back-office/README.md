# OE Back Office notes

This folder is the first compliance and admin register for **Oats, Etc.** (Modern Comfort Kitchen), Charles Warloe's Boise, Idaho food-business idea.

It is kept by OE Back Office. It is not the builder's work, not a kitchen scout, not a cost model, and not a brand or demand review. It has nothing to do with MLM Back Office or Mr. Life Manager. Do not mix those records with these.

**Date of this first build:** November 11, 2026.

**What this folder is not:** a permit, a legal opinion, a tax filing, an insurance quote, or permission to sell food. No one from this bot called an agency, filed a form, paid a fee, or agreed to terms.

## How to read this folder

Start here, in this order:

1. **[start-here.md](start-here.md)**: the lightest compliant path for a one-day trial, ordered by what actually blocks that trial, and what can wait.
2. **[this-week.md](this-week.md)**: a short list of what needs you this week (week of Wednesday, November 11, 2026, America/Boise). It matches start-here.md.
3. **[register.md](register.md)**: the master register of permits, licenses, registrations, insurance, tax obligations, and filings that are relevant to Stage 1.

Call scripts, a calendar and Drive plan, and a short questions list will be added in follow-up notes in this folder. Until those arrive, use the call outline in this-week.md, and the longer scripts already in `notes/oe-kitchen/` ([pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4)).

If you only have a few minutes on your phone, read start-here.md and this-week.md.

## Honesty conventions

These rules apply to every file in this folder.

- **UNVERIFIED** means the fact was not opened on an official source page during the November 11, 2026 research session, or the agency still has to classify your exact facts. Do not treat an UNVERIFIED line as a rule.
- **VERIFIED-this-session** means the official page was opened on November 11, 2026, and the note records only what that page said. The date checked is **2026-11-11**.
- If a fee, deadline, form number, or rule could not be read on an official page, the register says **not confirmed**. It does not invent a number.
- Regulations change. The agency's answer on your menu, site, and setup governs, not this folder.
- This work is not a substitute for a lawyer, accountant, or insurance agent. Items where a professional's opinion matters are flagged in the register (especially entity choice, tax treatment, and whether insurance is enough).
- Fees and hours quoted from city and state sites are in the form published on that date. Confirm them when you apply or call.

## What this first build is built on

Stage 1, in the project research archive, is a small break-even experiment: one plain cooked oatmeal base, a short breakfast menu with no eggs, one operator, and a target of paid bowls at one hosted private office or business site. Equipment, a kitchen, a parcel, a legal name, and licenses are not yet selected or approved. See `docs/oats-and-more/README.md` and `docs/oats-and-more/research/`.

This folder builds on work already in the repo and in open pull requests. It does not redo that work.

- Project research, especially `docs/oats-and-more/research/local-regulation.md`, `decision-log.md`, `pilot-and-equipment.md`, and `validation-backlog.md`.
- OE Growth Steward / OE Challenger notes in [pull request 3](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/3) (`notes/oe-challenger/`), including the point that where the oatmeal is cooked, and whether a one-day trial can happen before a full license, gate the first sale.
- OE Kitchen notes in [pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4) (`notes/oe-kitchen/`), especially the one-day trial checklist, kitchen leads, and deeper rules notes. Use those files for kitchen scouting and the longer event checklists. This folder adds the master register, the this-week list, and admin and tax items.
- OE Numbers notes were read from an in-progress local draft under `notes/oe-numbers/` on November 11, 2026. No OE Numbers pull request was open in `cwarloe/businessideas` at the time this folder was written. Those notes treat Stage 1 as a break-even learning experiment, leave license and insurance costs blank until you have a quote, and do not choose a permit path. This register does not copy their workbook or invent those costs.

Location, entity, and kitchen are still open. Items that depend on a specific parcel are marked **BLOCKED on location** in the register.
