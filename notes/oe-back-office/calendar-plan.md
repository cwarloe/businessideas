### After you have a tax permit, if you need one

- Temporary seller's permit for your own sales: file and pay within 15 days after the permit expires. Source: [Sales Tax: Filing and Paying](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/sales-use/stfiling/), opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session.
- Temporary seller's permit for one specific event: file and pay within 15 days after the event ends. Same source.
- Regular seller's permit: the Tax Commission assigns monthly, quarterly, semiannual, or annual filing. Most retailers file monthly, with tax due by the 20th of the following month. Retailers who owe less than $750 tax per quarter may be set up quarterly, due within 20 days after the end of the quarter. Add the actual due dates only after you receive the permit and the assigned schedule. Same source.
- You must file a return even if you made no sales, if you have a seller's permit. Same source.

## How to add these on Windows

1. In Edge or Chrome, go to [https://calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com) and sign in with the Google account you already use.
2. Create a calendar named something plain, such as "Oats Etc compliance," so these reminders do not sit on a personal calendar you share with other people by accident.
3. Use **Create**, then **Task** for calls and "apply by" dates. Use **Event** only for things that occupy a real time slot, such as the CDH call or the trial morning itself, once a date exists.
4. Set the event time zone to America/Boise when the deadline is an Idaho agency clock. Double-check the time on your phone afterward.
5. Turn on email or push notifications for 24 hours before and, for the 30-day application, one week before.
6. Do not invite an agency, a host, or a kitchen as a guest from this calendar unless you intend to send them a meeting.

## How to add these on an Android phone

1. Open the Google Calendar app, or open calendar.google.com in Chrome, with the same account.
2. Switch to the "Oats Etc compliance" calendar you created on the PC, or create it on the phone under **Settings**, then **Manage calendars**.
3. Add the CDH call as a timed event with a 15-minute notification. Add application-by dates as all-day tasks with a notification the morning of and the day before.
4. If you also use Samsung Calendar or another app, pick one system and stick to it so due dates are not split.
