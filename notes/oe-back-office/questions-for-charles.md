3. **Will the first trial collect money, or is it free samples only?** CDH may treat these differently. That answer is currently UNVERIFIED. Please also decide that you will not take paid preorders until CDH answers whether that is allowed before a license is issued.

4. **For the first trial, will you stay a sole proprietor, or do you want to form an LLC first?** This is a lawyer and accountant question, especially for a food business, because of personal liability. The lightest SOS path for using a trade name is often a sole proprietor with an assumed business name (published online fee $25). An LLC Certificate of Organization has a published online fee of $100. An assumed business name is not a license and not a liability shield. The City of Boise requires a current SOS registration that matches the name on a city license application. Do not file until you have chosen. Sources for the fees: [Idaho Secretary of State business forms](https://sos.idaho.gov/business-forms/), opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session.

5. **Is "Oats, Etc." the name you will put on applications?** The research archive treats this as a working name that has not been professionally cleared. You can call CDH with a working name. You should not file a city license, an assumed business name, or an entity until you are willing to use that name (or another name) on public records.
