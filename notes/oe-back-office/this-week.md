# What needs you this week

**Week of Wednesday, November 11, 2026 through Tuesday, November 17, 2026.**  
Times and office hours below are America/Boise (Mountain Time).

This list is short on purpose. It matches [start-here.md](start-here.md). The goal this week is to learn which license, if any, a one-day trial needs. It is not to file, pay, or announce a sale date.

You do not need to finish the whole register this week.

## Do these, in this order

1. **Decide, at least roughly, which one-day trial you would actually run first.** A paid booth at a public event, a paid morning at a private office, an employer-paid drop-off, or free samples only. You can describe more than one option on the same call. If you already have an address or a host in mind, write it down. If you do not, say that on the call. This is the only decision that has to happen before the first phone call.

2. **Call Central District Health and ask them to classify that trial.** This is the one call that should not slip to next week. Published questions number: (208) 375-5211. Ada and Boise Counties food line is also listed as (208) 327-7499 on the same CDH page. Source: [CDH food establishments](https://cdh.idaho.gov/licenses-permits-inspections/food-establishments/), opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session. Office hours were not confirmed on that page, so ask when they can call you back if you miss someone. Ask them to email the classification if they will. Write the answers down with the date and the person's name.

   A short outline you can use on the call: give your name, say you are planning a possible one-day oatmeal breakfast trial in the Boise area under the working name Oats, Etc., and that you are not operating yet. Describe plain cooked oatmeal with toppings such as brown sugar, cinnamon, raisins, maple, nuts, and possibly apple, with no eggs, held hot. Ask them to classify a paid one-day public-event booth, a paid one-morning stand on a private office lot, an employer-paid drop-off, and free samples only. For each, ask which license you need, whether you must cook in a licensed kitchen, what the fee and lead time are, whether you need a Certified Food Protection Manager, and whether you may take paid preorders before a license is issued. A longer script is in OE Kitchen's one-day trial checklist in [pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4).

3. **If CDH says you need a licensed kitchen, call two or three shared kitchens the same week if you still have time.** Do not book or pay a deposit yet unless you are sure of the trial type and date. Use the kitchen list and scripts already written in `notes/oe-kitchen/` ([pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4)). Published early leads in that scout were Gina's, Osage, and Flava or The Commons. Confirm hours, minimums, insurance, and whether they will sign a commissary agreement.

4. **If you already know the trial would be in Boise, call the City Clerk after the CDH call, not before.** City and zoning scripts are in OE Kitchen's checklist in [pull request 4](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/4). Published Clerk number: (208) 972-8150. Published hours: Monday through Wednesday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Thursday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., closed holidays (Mountain Time). Source: [City of Boise Clerk contact](https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing/other/vendor-license/), opened 2026-11-11, VERIFIED-this-session. If you do not yet know the city or the site, this call can wait until next week.

5. **Do not file, pay, sign, or advertise a paid date this week.** That includes Secretary of State filings, tax permits, city licenses, CDH applications, kitchen contracts, and insurance policies. After the CDH call, you will know which of those come next. If you want to prepare, you can search existing names on [SOSBiz](https://sosbiz.idaho.gov/). Searching is fine. Filing is not required this week.

## If you have leftover time

- Jot your trial type, any address you have, and whether the first trial will be paid or free, in a note on your phone or in a Word file on your Windows PC.
- If CDH or a kitchen told you an insurance limit, call your insurance agent with that number. If they have not, wait.
- Put a 30-day reminder on your calendar only after you have a proposed trial date and know you must apply that far ahead. Do not create a public event listing yet.

## What this week is not

This week is not the time to buy equipment, form an LLC on a deadline, or pick a recurring office host just to satisfy a form. Those can follow the classification. OE Challenger's notes in [pull request 3](https://github.com/cwarloe/businessideas/pull/3) are right that demand still has to be tested, but a paid sale still has to be lawful. The CDH call serves the lawful half.
