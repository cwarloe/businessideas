**Date checked:** November 11, 2026 (America/Boise).  
**Scope:** Commercial, prep, shared-use, and commissary kitchens in or near Boise, Meridian, Garden City, Eagle, and Nampa.  
**Status:** Advertised public information only. This scout did not call, email, book, or tour any kitchen. Rates, minimums, insurance, and hours must be confirmed with the kitchen before you rely on them. A rented kitchen is not, by itself, a license to sell food at an office or an event. See [one-day-trial-checklist.md](one-day-trial-checklist.md) and [rules-and-permits.md](rules-and-permits.md).

If Central District Health tells you that a one-day trial of hot oatmeal must be cooked in an approved facility, start with the kitchens that already publish an hourly rate and early or 24/7 access.

## How these compare for an Oats, Etc. pilot

The pilot needs short, early-morning cook and pack time, not a full-time restaurant lease. A two-hour cook slot a few mornings a week is the kind of use to ask about. Kitchen cost as a share of gross sales gets painful on very small batches. That math belongs to OE Numbers. This scout only flags the published hourly rates and the need to ask about minimums.

**First calls, if CDH requires a licensed kitchen:**

1. **Gina's Kitchen.** Simple published $40/hour rate with storage included and listed 24/7 access. Good fit to ask about 4:00 to 6:00 a.m. oatmeal batches. Confirm insurance, licenses, and whether they will sign a CDH commissary or approved-facility agreement.
2. **Osage Kitchen (Garden City).** Own-site $40/hour, early enough geographically for a Treasure Valley office run, and described as a licensed commissary. Confirm early hours (it is inside a bar building, so access hours matter), minimum booking, and commissary paperwork. Email first, because no verified phone was on the official site.
3. **Flava Kitchen.** Lowest published hourly rate ($30). Confirm whether the "4 hr" label is a minimum (a four-hour block would be $120 at the listed rate), early-morning access, and whether they accept a one-morning oatmeal operator.
4. **The Commons, Federal Way.** Most complete published commissary package, including 4 a.m. prime hours on the Kitchen Door listing, which matches an office breakfast prep window. Confirm operational status, current rates (own site asks you to request them), onboarding fees, and insurance limits. Monthly memberships at $2,200 and $3,600 are likely larger than a one-to-three-morning pilot needs unless you already know you will use the included hours.
5. **Season + Taste and Your Kitchen.** Worth a quote if the first three are a poor fit (hours, insurance, or downtown access). Do not assume they are cheaper or more expensive than $30 to $40/hour until they quote.

## Eagle and Nampa

No verified public listing for a shared commercial or commissary kitchen in Eagle or Nampa was found on November 11, 2026. If you want to sell or prep in Nampa, remember that Canyon County is Southwest District Health, not CDH. Ask SWDH whether they maintain a list of approved commissaries. Do not treat a random directory listing as proof that a kitchen exists.

A directory page for an "Aire Kitchen" in Boise with a $25/hour rate appeared in one third-party commissary index. No official Boise site, address, or local phone for that business was verified on this date. Treat **Aire Kitchen as UNVERIFIED** and do not plan around it until you can confirm a real facility.

The Kitchen Door listing previously cited for Osage ([thekitchendoor.com/kitchen-rental/osage-kitchen](https://www.thekitchendoor.com/kitchen-rental/osage-kitchen)) returned **404** on November 11, 2026. Do not use older third-party Osage rates ($35/$25 in an earlier project memo) without asking Osage. Their own site listed $40/hour on this date.

## What to ask every kitchen (short list)

Use the full script in [call-scripts.md](call-scripts.md). At minimum ask:

- Are you open and accepting new hourly users for early-morning oatmeal cook and pack (4:00 to 6:00 a.m.)?
- Current hourly or daily rate, minimum hours, onboarding or deposit fees, and what storage is included versus extra.
- Whether you may use kettles or stockpots for oatmeal, and what hot-hold or cooling equipment is on site.
- Insurance limits, additional-insured wording, business license, and food-manager certificate they require.
- Whether they will sign a Central District Health (or SWDH) commissary / approved-facility agreement.
- Parking, loading, 24/7 access method, and whether other tenants will be in the space at the same time.
- Cancellation rules and how you book (email, form, or app).

Log the answers in the kitchen table in [call-scripts.md](call-scripts.md).
