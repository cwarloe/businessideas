# Call scripts and question lists (drafts for Charles)

**Date:** November 11, 2026 (America/Boise).  
**These are drafts for you to use yourself.** This scout did not call, email, or submit forms to any kitchen, agency, or insurer.

Agency scripts for the one-day trial (CDH, City Clerk, Planning, ACHD, insurance, Secretary of State) live in the headline file: [one-day-trial-checklist.md](one-day-trial-checklist.md). Use those first. This file adds kitchen scripts, a longer question bank, and logs.

Call from your phone. Keep a notes app or a Word file open on your Windows PC. Ask for an email follow-up. If someone asks you to apply or pay during the call, you can say you are only gathering requirements today.

## Before any kitchen call

Have this ready:

- Working business name (Oats, Etc. is a working name, not a cleared legal name).
- Menu: plain cooked oatmeal, a few dry toppings, possible apple topping, no eggs in the pilot.
- What you need the room for: cook a batch, pack it, maybe cool or hot-hold, then load the Rogue. Typical window to ask about: 4:00 to 6:00 a.m. on one to three weekday mornings.
- Whether CDH has already told you that you need a commissary or approved-facility agreement. If you have not called CDH yet, call CDH first.
- Your insurance situation (even if it is "I will get a certificate once I know your limits").
- Your email address.

## Script G. Shared or commissary kitchen

Use this for Gina's, Osage, Flava, The Commons, Season + Taste, or Your Kitchen. Skip any question the website already answered, but still confirm the answer is current.

> Hello, my name is Charles Warloe. I am planning a small oatmeal breakfast service in the Boise area, still in the planning stage. I may need a licensed kitchen a few early mornings a week to cook and pack oatmeal, then take it to an office or an event in my SUV. I am not booking today. I need current rates, hours, and requirements.
>
> Questions:
> - Are you accepting new hourly users right now?
> - Can I book time between 4:00 and 6:00 a.m. on weekdays?
> - What is the current hourly rate? Is there a minimum number of hours per booking or per month?
> - Are there onboarding, deposit, application, or membership fees on top of the hourly rate? What is included in storage, and what costs extra?
> - May I cook oatmeal in stockpots or kettles on your range, and do you have space to stage warmers or to cool food if the health district requires it?
> - What insurance do you require (general liability, product liability, limits, additional insured wording)? What licenses or certificates do you require (business license, food manager, food handler)?
> - Will you sign a Central District Health commissary or approved-facility agreement if I need one for a temporary or mobile license?
> - How does access work at that hour (key, code, staff on site)? Is parking and loading available for an SUV?
> - How do I book, and what is the cancellation rule?
>
> My email is __________. Please send a rate sheet or a short email with those answers if you can.

## Longer question bank (if a call goes well)

Use only what you still need. Do not turn the first CDH call into an interrogation.

**CDH extras:** date-marking and leftover rules for oatmeal; whether fruit toppings change the classification; nut allergen expectations; whether an electric soup kettle may be used only for hot holding and not for cooking; whether a host break-room sink counts as a handwash station; wastewater disposal if you use a portable handwash jug; whether recurring Tue/Thu office service can ever stay on a TFE, or must move to mobile, intermittent, or catering; certified food protection manager deadline.

**City extras:** where the license must be posted; special-event permit versus vendor license; clean-up bond; whether employees of one company are "the public"; noise or generator rules (the pilot prefers host power, not a generator).

**Kitchen extras:** can you leave a labeled dry-storage bin; shared-allergen equipment rules; loading dock hours; sales tax on kitchen rent (UNVERIFIED, ask them and, if needed, the Tax Commission); who is present if something breaks at 5:00 a.m.

## After the calls

1. Put CDH's classification at the top of your notes. That answer controls whether you need a kitchen at all, and which license to buy.
2. Do not pay a kitchen deposit until CDH has said you need that kitchen and you have read the insurance requirement.
3. Do not file at the Secretary of State or the Tax Commission until you have chosen a name and a structure. Question lists are in the [one-day trial checklist](one-day-trial-checklist.md).
4. Keep this folder as a record. When an agency answers, add the date. If a published fee in these notes disagrees with what they tell you, believe the agency and write down what they said.
